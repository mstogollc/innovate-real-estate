Common Questions

Do these systems actually work, or is this a "power saver" gimmick?

The PowerHouse is a commercial-grade, UL 508 listed, U.S. patented system (Patent 11,165,250 B2) endorsed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers — it is not the residential "plug-in" scam category. Savings are measured against baseline per IPMVP Option B, the same protocol used for utility rebate programs. The water optimization valve is patented and NSF certified with over 30,000 installations. Both products carry manufacturer warranties.

What size facility does this make sense for?

Rule of thumb: if you have a 400-amp electrical service or larger, or your monthly water bill exceeds a few hundred dollars, there's usually a viable business case. Sweet-spot facilities are 20,000+ sq ft or spend $2,000+/month on either utility.

How long does installation take?

The water optimization valve is typically installed in a single day by a licensed plumber. The PowerHouse install is scheduled around your facility — most sites are online again the same day, and downtime is limited to the brief window needed to tie into the main electrical panel.

What kind of ROI should I expect?

Payback typically lands between 24 and 48 months depending on utility rate, load profile, and building type. After payback, savings drop straight to net operating income for the remaining 20+ years of equipment life.

Can I combine this with cost segregation or other tax strategies?

Yes. Equipment costs are capitalizable and generally fall into shorter-life recovery classes — a cost segregation study can accelerate the tax benefit further. Ask us on the discovery call and we'll loop in the right tax specialist for your situation.

Who installs it and who stands behind the warranty?

Installation is performed by licensed local trades under Innovate Real Estate's project management. The PowerHouse carries a 5-year manufacturer warranty with a 25-year design life; the water optimization valve carries a 10-year manufacturer warranty.
